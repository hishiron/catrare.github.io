Privacy Policy

This page explains how Catrare Tech ("we") handles personal and device information in the iOS app "Ride Metrics" ("the App").

1. Basic Policy

The App measures and records your bike rides, including route, speed, elevation, and calories burned. Ride data is stored on the device running the App first, and is never sent to any server we operate or to any third-party advertising or analytics provider. The App does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking SDK.

However, data may also be stored or sent outside the device (to Apple services, or to a destination you choose to share with) — but only if you explicitly enable the relevant feature yourself. See the sections below for details.

  • If you enable Health integration: recorded workouts are saved to Apple Health, and your body weight is read from Health for calorie calculations (Section 2)
  • If you enable Calendar integration: a summary of each ride is added to your calendar, which may sync to iCloud or another service depending on your calendar settings (Section 2)
  • If you use CSV/GPX export: wherever you choose to share the exported file (Section 3)

2. Device Information We Access and Why

The App accesses the following information only when the corresponding feature is enabled. None of this information is sent to our servers or to any third-party advertiser or analytics provider — it is used only for on-device measurement and display, and for saving to the optional integrations you enable (Apple Health / Calendar).

Location

Used to measure your route, speed, and elevation. While a ride is being recorded, location is used continuously — including while your screen is locked or the App is in the background — so tracking can continue. Recorded location data is stored on your device, and is only saved to Health (as part of a workout that includes the GPS route) if you have enabled Health integration.

Motion Sensors

Used to help detect automatic stops and resumes (such as waiting at a traffic light) and to record your riding state.

Health (Apple Health)

Accessed only if you enable Health integration, as follows:

  • Read: with your permission, body weight is read to improve the accuracy of calorie calculations.
  • Write: recorded rides are saved to Health as workouts, including distance, calories burned, and the GPS route.

Data saved to Health is managed according to Apple's Health features and privacy policy.

Calendar (Full Access)

Only if you enable Calendar integration, a summary of each recorded ride (time, distance, average speed, and calories burned) is added to your calendar as an event. The App reads your calendar to avoid duplicate entries and to determine whether an event should be updated or deleted, but it never adds location or place names to an event. Depending on your calendar settings, added events may sync to a calendar service such as iCloud.

3. Data Export

The App can export ride data as CSV or GPX files. Exported files may contain location data. If you choose to share an exported file outside the device or with a third party, how that data is handled afterward is the responsibility of the recipient.

4. Deleting Data

The effect of deleting data or uninstalling the App differs depending on where the data is stored, as described below.

In-App Data

Deleting a ride removes it from the App's on-device storage, and this cannot be undone by the App itself. Uninstalling the App from your device also permanently removes all ride records stored on the device. However, the App's data is included in standard iOS device backups (such as iCloud Backup or a local backup to a computer) by design. If a backup made before the deletion still exists, restoring that backup through iOS may also restore the deleted in-app data — this is a result of iOS's backup feature, not the App itself. Please manage or delete backups through the relevant backup feature (such as your iCloud settings).

Health and Calendar (Connected Services)

When you delete a ride that was synced via Health or Calendar integration, the App asks you separately whether to also delete the corresponding data from each connected service. If you choose not to, that ride's data remains in Health and/or Calendar (it is not automatically removed by deleting the ride in-app or uninstalling the App). You can delete Health data from the Health app, and Calendar events from the Calendar app, individually.

Exported Files

Files exported as CSV or GPX and then shared or saved are outside the App's control. They are not deleted when you delete a ride in the App or uninstall the App. Please delete them from wherever you shared or saved them, using that destination's own features.

5. Reviewing and Revoking Permissions

You can review or revoke access to Location, Motion & Fitness, Health, and Calendar at any time from the iOS Settings app (Settings > Privacy & Security, or Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices). Revoking a permission disables the corresponding measurement or integration feature, but does not affect ride records already stored on your device (data already saved to Health or Calendar is also not deleted just by revoking permission — see Section 4 for how to delete it).

6. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not provide or sell information obtained through the App to any third party.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features are added to or changed in the App. If we make a significant change, we will update this page to reflect it.
