iPhone App

Ride Metrics

A simple ride-tracking app that measures your route, speed, distance, and calories burned. Your data stays on your device, and only syncs with Health and Calendar if you choose to enable it.

Coming soon to the App Store Support

Features

Easy to read while riding, and detailed enough to review afterward.

Live Tracking

Current speed, average speed, distance, moving time, stopped time, elevation gain, and calories burned — all shown in large, glanceable text while you ride.

Speed Graph & Map View

Switch between a live speed graph and a map showing your current location and route.

Automatic Stop Detection

Automatically detects stops, like waiting at a traffic light, and excludes them from moving time and average speed.

Ride History & Stats

See details for every ride — distance, speed, elevation up/down, calories, and per-split speed — plus a monthly summary and a rolling summary of your last 10 rides.

Health & Calendar Integration

Optionally enable it to save workouts to Health and add ride summaries to your calendar.

CSV / GPX Export

Export your rides as CSV or GPX files to share with other apps and services.

Screenshots

Home, in-progress, history list, and ride detail screens.
